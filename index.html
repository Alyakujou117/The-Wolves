Rules of The Wolves

Game Objective

The Villagers must identify and banish all Wolves before the Wolves equal or outnumber the remaining Villagers.

Roles Legend

Wolves

Eliminate one player each night. Blend in during day discussions.

Seer (Detective)

Inspect one player each night to discover if they are a Wolf or Innocent.

Healer (Doctor)

Protects players from Wolf attacks. Subject to custom doctor settings in setup.

Hunter (Guard)

Takes a night turn to shoot. If they target a Wolf, the Wolf is eliminated! If eliminated, they fire a final revenge shot.

Villager

No special night powers. Use debate and logic to spot liar behavior and vote correctly.
